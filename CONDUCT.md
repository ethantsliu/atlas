# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We welcome contributors and users regardless of background, identity, experience,
or affiliation. Participate with empathy, technical honesty, and respect. Give
specific, constructive feedback; distinguish evidence from inference; credit prior
work; and protect private or unpublished information encountered during review.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate humiliation, sexualized attention,
doxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Publishing another person's
private contact details, local paths, credentials, review identity, or unpublished
materials without permission is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project repositories, reviews, issue discussions, and
project-related public spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers
through the repository owner's contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical, subject to safety and legal duties.
